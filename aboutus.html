ABOUT SANSKRITI CANVAS

A digital canvas for India's living heritage.

Sanskriti Canvas is a digital platform created to bring India's rich heritage, traditions, art, festivals and cultural stories together in one beautiful space.

OUR STORY

Preserving the past. Inspiring the future.

India is home to one of the world's oldest and most diverse civilizations. From magnificent monuments and ancient temples to folk traditions, crafts, food and festivals, every region has a story waiting to be discovered.

Yet, discovering this enormous cultural wealth can often be difficult. Sanskriti Canvas was imagined to make cultural exploration simpler, more engaging and accessible through a modern digital experience.

Our platform connects people with India's heritage through interactive exploration, curated stories and meaningful cultural experiences.

OUR VISION

One platform. Infinite stories.

We envision a digital India where anyone can explore the country's heritage and culture — from a famous monument to a small local tradition — through one connected cultural experience.

BUILT WITH PURPOSE

Built by students. Inspired by India.

Sanskriti Canvas is a student-driven project created with a simple belief: technology can help preserve and celebrate culture in meaningful ways.

By combining modern web technology with India's timeless cultural identity, we want to create an experience that feels both familiar and future-ready.
